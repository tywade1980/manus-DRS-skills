# Houzz Pro Financial Management Workflow

This reference outlines the procedures for managing invoices, payments, and financial tracking within Houzz Pro.

## 1. Invoicing and Billing

### Converting Estimates to Invoices
1.  Once an Estimate is approved and work is ready to commence (or a milestone is reached), navigate to the **Invoices** section of the Project.
2.  Do not create invoices from scratch if an estimate exists. Always convert the approved Estimate into an Invoice to maintain data consistency.
3.  **Progressive Invoicing:** For large projects, customize the payment terms based on a percentage of completion or specific project milestones.

### Change Orders
When the project scope shifts (e.g., client requests an upgrade, or unforeseen issues arise):
1.  Do not edit the original approved Estimate.
2.  Navigate to **Change Orders** within the Project.
3.  Create a new Change Order detailing the specific additions or subtractions to the scope and cost.
4.  Ensure Wade approves the Change Order before sending it to the client for signature.

### Retainers
For initial deposits:
1.  Create a **Retainer** within the Project.
2.  Once the retainer is paid, apply it as a credit against future Invoices.

## 2. Payments and Financing

### Online Payments
Ensure clients are aware of the available payment methods configured in `https://pro.houzz.com/manage/py/online/setup`:
*   **Credit or Debit Card:** 3% transaction fee, 0.5% platform fee.
*   **Bank Transfer:** 0.6% transaction fee, 0.4% platform fee.

### Client Financing
If a client expresses concern about project costs, remind Wade that he can offer financing options directly through Houzz Pro:
*   **Affirm:** Buy Now Pay Later (up to $30K limit).
*   **GreenSky:** Homeowner Loans.
*   **Figure:** HELOC Financing.

## 3. Accounting and Analysis

### QuickBooks Integration
Houzz Pro is integrated with QuickBooks Online.
*   Invoices, payments, and expenses logged in Houzz Pro will automatically sync to QuickBooks.
*   Do not manually duplicate data entry between the two systems.

### Budgeting
*   Regularly check the **Budget** and **Financial Overview** sections within a Project to compare estimated costs versus actual spends and monitor ongoing profitability.
